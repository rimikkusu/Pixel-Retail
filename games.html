Notes from the game counter

Each title keeps its original shelf tips, features, and playing steps.

Resident Evil 2 — PlayStation, 1998 · $2.99 for 3 days

Leon and Claire reach Raccoon City on a very bad night. Supplies are limited, so sometimes running past a monster is the smart move.

Developer: Capcom · Publisher: Capcom · Age rating: Mature (17+)

Features

  • Two character stories
  • Puzzle solving
  • Classic survival horror

How to play

Keep moving through Raccoon City:

  1. Search for useful items
  2. Read documents for clues
  3. Find a route to safety

Metal Gear Solid — PlayStation, 1998 · $2.99 for 3 days

Play as Solid Snake, keep an eye on the radar, and try not to walk straight into a guard. The cardboard box is more useful than it looks.

Developer: Konami Computer Entertainment Japan · Publisher: Konami · Age rating: Mature (17+)

Features

  • Stealth-based missions
  • Memorable boss encounters
  • A long story told through radio calls

How to play

Complete the mission:

  1. Use cover and watch patrols
  2. Collect equipment
  3. Reach the target undetected

Tekken 3 — PlayStation, 1998 · $2.99 for 3 days

What to try

  • A good-sized roster to pick from.
  • One-on-one fights with plenty of different moves.
  • Practice mode is useful before challenging a friend.

How to play

  1. Choose a fighter you like.
  2. Try a few moves from the list.
  3. Plug in a second controller for versus mode.

Final Fantasy VII — PlayStation, 1997 · $2.99 for 3 days

What to try

  • A long story with turn-based battles.
  • Materia lets you change how your party fights.
  • There are plenty of side roads if you need a break from saving the world.

How to play

  1. Follow the story out of Midgar.
  2. Try different Materia in your party.
  3. Save often; the rental copy will not remember for you.

Silent Hill 2 — PlayStation 2, 2001 · $3.99 for 3 days

James follows a letter into a foggy town. Bring some patience; the map is more useful than it looks.

Developer: Team Silent · Publisher: Konami · Age rating: Mature (17+)

Features

  • Walk the foggy streets and check your map
  • Puzzles and clues
  • Listen for sounds that warn you something is nearby

How to play

Explore the town:

  1. Read the map and conserve supplies
  2. Investigate places named in the letter
  3. Solve puzzles and discover what happened

Grand Theft Auto III — PlayStation 2, 2001 · $3.99 for 3 days

Liberty City is big, and the radio keeps the drives from getting quiet. Watch your wanted level before heading back to the garage.

Developer: DMA Design · Publisher: Rockstar Games · Age rating: Mature (17+)

Features

  • Drive around Liberty City
  • Find the next job on the map
  • Radio stations

How to play

Start with a safehouse:

  1. Visit a safehouse
  2. Choose a mission
  3. Follow the objective on your map

Gran Turismo 3: A-Spec — PlayStation 2, 2001 · $3.99 for 3 days

What to try

  • Start in Arcade mode if you just want to race.
  • There are cars to unlock and tune.
  • A second controller makes the races more fun.

How to play

  1. Choose a car that handles well for you.
  2. Brake before the corner, not in the middle of it.
  3. Ask before borrowing a memory card.

Super Mario 64 — Nintendo 64, 1996 · $2.99 for 3 days

Mario gets into the castle by jumping into paintings. Look behind things and around corners for Power Stars.

Developer: Nintendo EAD · Publisher: Nintendo · Age rating: Everyone

Features

  • Big rooms to poke around in
  • Power Star challenges
  • Familiar faces around the castle

How to play

For your first few stars:

  1. Enter a painting
  2. Explore and complete a challenge
  3. Return to the castle with your Star

GoldenEye 007 — Nintendo 64, 1997 · $2.99 for 3 days

What to try

  • Check the mission list before charging ahead.
  • Split-screen is best with a second controller.
  • The guards are not always looking the way you expect.

How to play

  1. Choose a mission and read the objectives.
  2. Look around before you enter a room.
  3. Try multiplayer if somebody else is over.

Sonic Adventure 2 — Dreamcast, 2001 · $2.99 for 3 days

What to try

  • Two teams, with different kinds of levels.
  • Sonic levels are about speed; Knuckles searches for pieces.
  • The Chao Garden is there if you have time to spare.

How to play

  1. Pick Hero or Dark to start.
  2. Follow the on-screen prompts for each character.
  3. Take your time in the garden between levels.

Tony Hawk’s Pro Skater 2 — Dreamcast, 2000 · $2.99 for 3 days

What to try

  • Career mode for goals and quick runs.
  • Build a combo, but remember to land it.
  • Free Skate is good for learning a new park.

How to play

  1. Pick a skater and take a look around the park.
  2. Try a few tricks and link them into a combo.
  3. Land before the timer runs out.

Halo: Combat Evolved — Xbox, 2001 · $3.99 for 3 days

The campaign takes you to a ring-shaped world. If someone else is over, try a split-screen match after the first mission.

Developer: Bungie · Publisher: Microsoft Game Studios · Age rating: Mature (17+)

Features

  • A full campaign to play through
  • Split-screen multiplayer
  • Strange ring world, lots of open space

How to play

Getting started:

  1. Learn the controls
  2. Follow your squad
  3. Reach each mission objective

Max Payne — PC, 2001 · $2.99 for 3 days

What to try

  • A rainy New York crime story.
  • The slow-motion move is handy when a room gets busy.
  • Ammo goes quickly, so look around between fights.

How to play

  1. Read the comic panels; they carry the story.
  2. Use cover and watch your ammo.
  3. Save when you find a phone.

Diablo II — PC, 2000 · $2.99 for 3 days

What to try

  • Pick a character class before heading out.
  • Explore dungeons and collect better gear.
  • Keep an eye on your health; the road back is long.

How to play

  1. Choose a character and set up your skills.
  2. Click monsters to fight and items to pick them up.
  3. Save your progress before returning the disc.
