PIXEL RENTALS WEB-READY DOODLE PACK

This pack is prepared for a desktop school website designed around a 1366x768 browser viewport and about 1200px of main content width.

IMPORTANT
- The images inside web_ready_assets are already at their intended browser pixel dimensions.
- To keep them exactly as drawn, do not resize them in CSS and do not add width/height attributes in HTML.
- Example: <img src="images/decorations/arrow_1.png" alt="">
- Pixel dimensions matter for websites. Photoshop DPI/PPI does not change their intended browser size.
- Transparent space is intentional where needed so the artwork keeps its original proportions instead of being stretched.

Folders
- web_ready_assets: use these in the website.
- source_full_resolution: the larger generated source files, kept in case you want to edit/re-export later.
- drawing_resolutions_and_uses.txt: exact natural sizes and suggested roles.
